# Kana Quiz — Visual Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
|---|---|---:|
| Ink Observatory | A contemplative Japanese study instrument with midnight ink surfaces, constellated kana, and restrained cyan light. It should feel precise, focused, and quietly premium. | 0.06 |
| Paper Lantern Studio | A warm editorial study space inspired by washi paper, vermilion marks, and softly lit library shelves. It balances familiarity with craft. | 0.03 |
| Signal Dojo | A kinetic training interface with high-contrast utility panels, electric status indicators, and a rhythmic modular system. It is energetic without becoming a neon game UI. | 0.08 |

## Chosen approach: Ink Observatory

### Design Movement

**Japanese editorial minimalism meets astronomical instrument design.** The interface combines the discipline of a reference book with the quiet dimensionality of a night observatory. It is explicitly not a generic cyberpunk dashboard.

### Core Principles

1. **Focused study over spectacle:** large kana, clear answers, and immediate status feedback remain visually dominant.
2. **Measured luminosity:** cyan and violet light is used as an informational signal, never as uncontrolled decoration.
3. **Layered material depth:** ink-black surfaces, translucent panels, fine rules, and selective grain create tactility without visual clutter.
4. **Intentional asymmetry:** the header and feature panels use offset composition, while the quiz grid remains functional and regular.

### Color Philosophy

The base palette is near-black indigo and blue-charcoal, intended to reduce glare during sustained study. Icy cyan is the signature signal for selection and forward progress, blue-violet indicates neutral navigation, jade confirms correct recall, and coral red highlights mistakes. Text uses warm porcelain rather than pure white to retain a book-like calm.

### Layout Paradigm

The application uses an **instrument panel and reading table** structure: a compact top rail, an offset hero and control area, a vertical signal spine for configuration, and a broad study canvas for the quiz deck. The quiz cards form a responsive working surface rather than a centered marketing grid.

### Signature Elements

1. A small orbital **kana crest** built from circles and a central Japanese glyph.
2. Fine **constellation rules** and luminous position dots along section edges.
3. A translucent **progress spectrum** that turns selection and accuracy into visible signals.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interaction should feel like operating a refined learning instrument. Selected controls receive a precise edge light; input feedback is immediate and unambiguous; the next unanswered card receives a calm focus halo. There are no gratuitous sounds, excessive particles, or cartoon-like reward effects.

### Animation

Panels enter with a short upward fade, selection glows settle within 180ms, and completion states use a single 220ms pulse. The visual background drifts almost imperceptibly. All nonessential animation must be disabled for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System

`Space Grotesk` provides confident display typography for headings and interface numerals. `Inter` supports compact functional copy. `Noto Sans JP` and `Noto Serif JP` are dedicated kana choices, selected by the learner. Headings use generous tracking and high contrast; labels are uppercase, small, and spaced.

### Brand Essence

**Kana Quiz is a focused Japanese-script recall instrument for learners who want deliberate, distraction-free practice.**

Personality: **precise, contemplative, encouraging**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are direct and study-oriented; CTAs are clear, specific, and calm. Microcopy recognizes effort without exaggeration.

Example lines: `Choose the sounds you want to strengthen.` and `One clear recall at a time.`

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark is an **orbital kana crest**: a luminous ring split by a small alignment tick, with the kana あ at its center. The adjacent wordmark uses a wide, compact display treatment rather than a default text treatment.

**Observatory Cyan — #67E8F9**
